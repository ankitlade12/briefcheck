# BriefCheck scope
## User direction
User supplied BriefCheck_Product_Requirements.docx and explicitly requested building all features, including the full roadmap, on October 25, 2026. This approval covers product scope; it does not claim a completed curriculum interview or final hands-on review.
## The Unique Kernel
Evidence → example → organizer decision → local edit → retest. Every claim links to an immutable source version and numeric outcomes use ordinary code.
## Audience
Hackathon organizers and operations reviewers; participant-facing approved clarifications and judge rubric alignment are secondary workflows.
## The POC Boundary
Deliver a working review cycle first, then continue full requested scope. P0: 2–4 documents, 40,000 characters, requirements extraction, supported comparisons, boundary tests, decisions, versioned drafts, undo, HTML/JSON reports.
## Expanded scope
P1: controlled public URL and text PDF imports, persistent workspaces, reviewer roles, revision history, coverage and baseline comparison.
P2: authorized workspace integrations, reusable templates, multiple languages and advanced conditional rules. Unsupported semantics must remain unresolved; no invented eligibility verdicts.
## Explicitly Cut
Nothing in the requested feature list is silently cut. OCR, automatic external publishing, legal adjudication, winner selection and plagiarism detection are outside the source PRD.
## Inspiration & Identity
Independent BriefCheck branding, editorial light interface, dark ink, teal actions, legible evidence, no readiness score or Devpost endorsement.
## Proof of success
New pasted text produces grounded findings; 179/180/181-second tests expose a boundary; a local edit creates a version and changes only relevant outcomes; exports retain the audit trail.
